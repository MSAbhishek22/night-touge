## Short description (paste-ready)

> Night Touge is a 3D endless driver on a mountain highway at 2 a.m.
> Hold a rising cruise speed, thread the gaps in night traffic, and
> get paid for passing close — near misses are the score. The left
> lane is oncoming: bigger bonuses, shorter runs. One crash ends it.
> How long do you stay lucky?

## Long description (paste-ready)

> A night highway, a low coupe, and traffic that does not move for you.
>
> Night Touge is a complete little arcade driver built in Three.js.
> Your car holds a cruise speed that climbs the longer you survive.
> Same-direction traffic dawdles in two lanes; a third lane runs
> oncoming, lit by its own headlights. Steering is the game: slide
> between cars with less than a car's width to spare and the run pays
> a near-miss bonus — chain them for up to ×5, with an extra cut for
> oncoming passes. Touch anything and the run is over: score, distance,
> near-miss count, and your best score on the board.
>
> The night is the other half of the game. Sodium streetlights pool on
> the asphalt, a lit city glows off the right shoulder, hills and a
> moon close the horizon, and past about 120 km/h the world starts to
> streak. Sound is synthesized live — an engine hum that follows your
> speed, a blip for every near miss — no audio files, no downloads,
> no login. It runs as plain static files and lowers its own effects
> if your laptop asks it to.
>
> Built from scratch during the jam: every car is Three.js primitives,
> the road is a pooled, bending ribbon of recycled segments, and the
> whole game is under 1.5 MB including the engine.

- P — pause · M — sound on/off · R — restart after a crash
